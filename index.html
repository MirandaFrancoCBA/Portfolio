About

I care about what software does after it leaves localhost.

I'm Franco Rodrigo Miranda, a software developer based in Córdoba, Argentina. I focus on backend engineering with Python and Django while working comfortably across frontend, mobile and deployment when a product needs end-to-end ownership.

My recent work includes a deployed inventory system, a real-time collaborative task application and a full-stack e-commerce platform. Across them I have worked with relational data modeling, authentication and authorization, transactional workflows, WebSockets, automated testing, Docker and CI/CD.

I'm completing a Higher Technical Degree in Software Development, with previous university studies in Information Systems Engineering. My earlier experience in technical sales, teaching and production also shapes how I communicate, collaborate and approach practical user needs.
